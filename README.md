# dgd-design-portfolio -- After-Glade

GDD Part 1 final: After-Glade

Press the W key to begin your journey! After getting another eviction notice on your personal music studio--you probably have a million by now--you decide its best to stay at your friends hangar. Your anthropomorphic eagle friend understands what your going through after you explain it to him. He wants to help you out--so he takes you on his stunt plane. 

Travel through regions such as cloudy-deserts or even rainy-graveyards. You prefer peace being a dragon-cat--but you fortunately aren't alone on this journey. Meet a new anti-hero who helps you out with your eviction notice--by supplying you peanut butter jars throughout each region. You find out the jelly isn't such a nuisance after all..

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This game can be compared to Sonic The Hedgehog. After-Glade is a platformer/racing hybrid. Recommended for teens of starting skills.

You can win each stage by reaching the finish line. When you drive in jelly (acts as oil) you will slide and lose a spoon. Lose all spoons and you restart at the last checkpoint. Each stage will contain a certain amount of spoons to replenish your status.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Initial asset list:
UI: main menu, pause menu, peanut butter collect animation, stunt points, special points, and region complete score.

Hazards: jelly spill, jelly-throwing catapoult.

Other: moving background, collection booth, region train, peanut butter jar, rock/ambient region music, main menu/pause music, and air fan.

Characters: G-Caps (dragon/cat hybrid character), Bagel (eagle character), Underscored (deer character)

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Mechanics: there are ramp jumps, driving, jam spills, and air floating.

Dynamics: doing tricks off the ramp while airborne, and drifting in the jelly to get a short speed boost.

Art Style: low saturation--warm colors; the background is a little greyed-out. motorcycle will have a purple emphasis. Camera is top-down (known as 3/4 angle) to help look for future obstacles. Animation principles like exaggeration will be used for the speeding up of the motorcycle. Game is about the characters growing, finding peace, and playfulness. I'm aiming for a similar art style to Sonic Frontiers. Animations will look choppy (24 fps) on 'ones' and slower paced scene have 'twos' (12 fps) (otherwise to make a 12 fps work needs two of the same exact frame twice) Original idea for characters was for them to appear pixelated while the background was hand-drawn, but I decided its going to be the opposite. I think it will save me a lot of time that way.

Aesthetic: player feels expression because they get to create their own tricks.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Backstory: G-Caps is trying to pay off his eviction notice that he recently got, which he is motivated by his dad's death to reclaim his father's studio. He is excellent at predicting future hazards right before they happen. He isn't so great at driving his motorcycle. He normally goes for the longer option, even though he is offered the easy way. Most of the easy ways end up to a hazard. He was going to apply for the company to deliver packages, but inside them contained illegal assets--in which Bagel told him about when he got fired.

Underscored has had a rough life growing up--always was manipulated and treated badly. He makes a promise with himself and wants to not let this happen to anyone. Underscored is very socially awkward and wears a mask. He finds G-Caps is different than the rest of society and chooses him as his first person to help. When people return the offer to him, he often is a little hesitant, but accepts it anyway despite his uncomfort.

Bagel came from a previous job about building machinery inside the companies hangar, only to find out they were purchasing illegal assets in their spare time. He found this wrong and bought his own hangar, resulting in him losing his last paycheck, which could've been life changing. He met G-Caps, which he was about to apply for the company, but told him not to. They became really great friends.
