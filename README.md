# dgd-design-portfolio -- After-Glade

After-Glade is a game about riding a motorcycle through fast-platformer-based stages: ranging from cloudy-deserts to rainy-graveyards. Your goal is to collect all sorts of peanut butter jars and spend them to upgrade your bike. Maybe you just want to chill: you can explore a small city with all sorts of secrets and fun stuff. A train will be present in every level. Occasionally, a ramp will appear. You can ride on the ramp in order to get on top of the train: find special peanut butter jars on the roof of the train. Inactivity will result in your motorcycle and you to slowly descend down the ramp again. This is physics in a way because the train is going to be moving.

This game can be compared to Sonic The Hedgehog, but keeps it originality. After-Glade is going to be a platformer and racing game at the same time due to riding on a motorcycle of course, and lastly from jumping from ramps and the stunts. This game will be recommended for teens and those of pre-adolescence with starter-to-advanced skill levels. The player hits jumps in order to get special points (separate from normal score) 

You can win each stage by reaching the finish line, which depending on the size will have more checkpoints, as for convenience. When you drive in jelly (acts as oil) you will slide and lose a spoon. Lose all spoons and you restart at the last checkpoint. Each stage will contain more spoons to replenish your status.

Initial asset list: main menu background, peanut butter collect animation, stunt points UI, moving background non-pixelated, pixelated characters, collection booth of peanut butter jars, in-game badges, and non-linear decisions you can make.

Mechanics: there are jumps, driving, and jam spills at the moment.

Dynamics: airborne tricks right after you jump off the ramp, and drifting in jam to get a slight speed boost.

Aesthetic: player feels expression due to creating their own combo trick sets.

A challenger emerges. He will first be oblivious to the player until later on. He will occasionally get stuck in spills. Help him and then you will receive a badge.
